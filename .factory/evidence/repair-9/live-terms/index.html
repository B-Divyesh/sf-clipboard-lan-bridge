These terms govern Clipboard LAN Bridge. Do not use the software if you do not agree.

The software

Clipboard LAN Bridge transfers short text between paired devices on a local network. It is not a password manager, backup system, or emergency service.

You are responsible for the text you send. Confirm the destination and compare the pairing code.

License and acceptable use

The source code uses the MIT License. Do not break laws, interfere with networks, bypass access controls, or contact devices without permission.

Free plan and license

The free plan connects this computer and one paired device. Transfers expire after two or ten minutes.

An existing license removes the paired-device limit and enables one-hour transfers. Restore it by pasting its token in the desktop app.

New licenses are not available.

Security and availability

No software is risk-free. Keep your operating system current, compare pairing codes, and use networks you trust.

Firewalls, guest Wi-Fi isolation, VPNs, and sleeping devices may prevent delivery. Keep the phone page open until the transfer arrives. The software is provided “as is” where law permits.
